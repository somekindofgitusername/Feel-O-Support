Privacy Policy for See-O
Last Updated: June 2, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how See-O ("the App") handles your data. Our core principle is privacy by design: See-O is a local-only application that does not collect, track, or share your personal data.
1. Data Collection and Usage
See-O is a perceptual training tool designed to operate entirely offline on your iOS device.
No Data Collection: We do not collect, track, or store any of your data on external servers.  
ZIP
Local Processing: All visual stimuli are procedurally generated in real-time on your device using Metal, and all training data is processed locally.  
ZIP
No Analytics: We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking software.  
ZIP
2. Data Persistence (SwiftData)
The App uses local storage to manage your training progress.
Local Storage: All data, including SessionRecord, TrialData, and UserStats, is stored locally on your device using SwiftData.  
ZIP
User Control: Because all information is stored locally, your data remains entirely under your control and resides only on your device.  
ZIP
Data Security: The security of your data is governed by the built-in security features of your iOS device.
3. Network Usage
No Network Calls: See-O does not perform any network calls or cloud syncing. The App is fully functional without an internet connection.  
ZIP
4. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information or training data to outside parties. We have no mechanism to share your data, as the App does not connect to the internet.  
ZIP
5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
